- PvP formation: `pvpBattleGoal` returns a `follow` goal whenever the AI is farther than its vocation ring (`pref=min(pvpApproachRange, PVP_BATTLE_FOLLOW_RANGE=5)`: knight/monk hold 1 sqm, ranged hold 3) from `pvpBattleLeaderChar` — the DRIVEN char (`_pvpDrivenAt` within 5s) of the leader's account, falling back to the first living char; the anchor never follows. Only the client-steered char (`visual.self` from `account-client.js`) marks `_pvpDrivenAt` in `syncAuthorityVisualState`: other chars of the same account are server AI — never apply their echoed pose (it arrives a tick late and regresses AI steps). A follower inside any safezone ignores the engagement gate and marches out (PZ forbids attacking from inside); `pvpFollowCell` skips enemy-sz cells and prefers cells outside any safezone. The follow goal is a specific free reachable cell in the ≤5 ring (`pvpFollowCell`, scored nearest-to-follower), not the leader's cell — followers spread around the ring instead of queueing into a corridor. A follower only engages a hostile when the target is within `FOLLOW_RANGE + approachRange` of the leader (melee 6, ranged 8): farther targets mean hold the line, no chase. A designated `teams[t].targetId` short-circuits formation: the whole team pushes that target (melee to 1 sqm, ranged to 3) with no engagement gate. Approach distance is vocation-based in `pvpApproachRange`: knight/monk always melee (1 sqm), paladin/druid/sorcerer hold 3 sqm, unknown voc falls back to min(weapon, spell reach). The idle formation ring uses the same vocation distance — `pvpFollowCell` scores each candidate by `|d - want|` where `want=min(alcance,5)` (EK/monk hug at 1 sqm, ranged form a second line at 3) and falls back to neighbor rings up to 5 when full. Too-close retreat uses `pvpStepToRange` (any free step toward the ring, incl. lateral/diagonal — plain `authorityStepAway` deadlocked RPs in crowds); `pvpStepToward` sidesteps allied blockers. In PvP the per-second step window is 5 (`stepCap`), not 1 — otherwise bots crawl at 1 sqm/s while manual chars walk ~5x faster. The movement consumer must honor the goal's own `td` (a ring cell carries `td:0` = step ONTO it): `Number(goal.td)||alcance` collapses 0 to 1 and froze every follower exactly 1 sqm short of its cell — check `goal.td` with `Number.isFinite` instead. `advanceAuthorityMovement`'s player loop freezes only the client's `self` chars (`visual.self`, `pvpFrozenIds`): freezing on ANY submitted `visual.players` silenced every bot, which is why only real-account chars ever dealt damage.
